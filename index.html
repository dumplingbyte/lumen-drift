Lumen Drift

Let go at the right moment.

A mote of light circles a quiet world. One tap sends it into the dark, carried by momentum toward the next luminous orbit. Thread quick captures into a Drift Chain, find the perfect release, and stay above the rising dusk.

  • One-thumb controls with instant retries
  • Three modes: Drift, Daily Drift and Zen
  • Eight palettes to earn, plus the original night sky
  • Optional Game Center leaderboards and 12 achievements
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no account required

Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store, and for Android.
