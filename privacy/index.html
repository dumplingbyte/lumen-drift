Privacy Policy

Lumen Drift for iPhone, iPad and Android · Effective 13 October 2026

Lumen Drift is published by Britanica Limited (company no. 12863970). On the App Store the developer is shown as Dumpling Byte. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app (bundle ID com.lumendrift.app) and the Android app (package uk.co.britanica.lumendrift).

What we collect

Nothing. Lumen Drift does not collect or share personal data. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking, developer-operated accounts, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Britanica Limited does not receive gameplay logs, scores or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Scores, progress, unlocked palettes, daily attempts and settings are stored locally on your device. Reset Progress in the game's Settings erases this local data. Deleting the app also removes it, subject to your device's backup settings.

Apple Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

Game Center is optional. When it is available and enabled through your Apple account, the game sends leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center service. Apple operates that service under its own terms and privacy policy. Lumen Drift does not receive your Apple account credentials, and Britanica Limited does not receive this data. Resetting progress in the game does not delete records held by Game Center. The Android version has no online leaderboards.

Purchases

If you buy Lumen Drift from Apple's App Store or Google Play, the store processes the purchase under its own terms and privacy policy. The game does not receive your payment details.

Permissions

The game is fully playable without an internet connection. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo-library access.

Children

Lumen Drift is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will appear on this page. Questions: anica@britanica.co.uk, Britanica Limited.
