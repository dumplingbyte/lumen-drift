Help for Lumen Drift on iPhone, iPad and Android

Playing

Tap anywhere while orbiting to release. The light continues along the tangent until another body's capture ring catches it. Quick captures build a chain. Release inside the bright arc for a Perfect. Avoid the screen edges and the rising dusk.

Daily Drift gives every player the same route for the UTC date. Your first daily attempt is official; later attempts are practice. Zen removes the rising dusk and submits no scores.

Sound and motion

Settings has separate Music, Sound effects and Haptics switches. Reduce Motion limits parallax, screen shake and particle effects, and the game also follows your device's Reduce Motion setting. Left-handed pause moves the pause button to the other side.

Records and Game Center

Progress is saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad, Game Center is optional and appears only when available. If leaderboards do not appear, check Game Center in iOS Settings. You can keep playing without signing in.

Reset Progress erases local records, earned palettes and daily history. Game Center records remain. The game asks for confirmation first.

Contact
